# Convoy title candidate

Active title resource, connected through `TITLE_IMG` in `GetResourceName`.

Created with the built-in imagegen tool using actual game screenshots as references:

- `output/stage-combat-review/combat.png`: wooden starter castle and catapult.
- `output/fixed-width-review/castle-04-base.png`: turtle castle and Gatling cannon.
- `output/uniform-castles-review-900/castle-07-stage-0.png`: black/red castle and crystal cannon.
- `output/uniform-castles-review-900/castle-10-stage-0.png`: white/blue castle and blue/gold cannon.

Prompt brief: Deliberately chunky low-resolution pixel-art title composition. Preserve the four referenced castle silhouettes, emblems, colors and artillery. Arrange them as a convoy on one railway receding into the distance, with small varied characters on exterior decks and a looming demon king behind the convoy. Replace the first-stage forest with a restrained navy/purple night sky. CASTLE SURVIVOR pixel logo at top. No buttons or inventory UI.

The castles are generated interpretations of the reference sprites, not pixel-identical copies of the source assets.
