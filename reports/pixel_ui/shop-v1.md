# Pixel shop concept

`shop-v1.png` is a visual proposal generated with built-in imagegen, based on `output/pixel-shop-review/shop-top.png`.

Prompt brief: Preserve the top resource strip and icon-only navigation. Fill the area between them with a compact pixel shop header, featured offer, categories and a three-column product grid. Use navy/teal stepped panels, bitmap Korean type and limited gold accents. Remove ornate painted signage and ribbons. Selected shop navigation occupies 28% width. Show the colorful portal behind a small lower-right padlock.

Illustrated amounts and prices are layout examples, not changes to the live product catalog. The pixel shop appearance is now implemented with native panels, compact header, category shortcuts, featured offer, compact castle link and product cards. The shop owns all space below the resource strip, covers the bottom navigation and removes its touch areas until closed. Live product names, amounts, prices and purchase handling remain data-driven.
