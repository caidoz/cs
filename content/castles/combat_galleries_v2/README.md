# Integrated combat galleries

Ten newly authored lower fighting galleries replace the earlier floating gap,
stretched posts and standalone deck strip. Each bitmap contains a solid dark
rear wall, thick ceiling and floor beams, load-bearing end columns and knee
braces as one connected architectural module. Foreground companion space is
clear of middle posts and railings. Materials follow all ten castle exteriors.

Generated with built-in imagegen. `originals` contains untouched outputs;
`generation-prompts.json` records the exact prompts and construction reference.
The initial complete wooden-castle design is saved in
`../timber_combat_gallery_v1/castle-01-complete.png`.

Runtime originals are copied unchanged into `Resources/res/castle_combat_galleries`.
The registrar measures sprite bounds and records the floor at 80% of height.
The renderer fits the complete module between upper hull and chassis, overlaps
its thick top beam with the hull underside and aligns its floor with the lower
chassis. No slivers from other artwork are stretched into support posts.
Companion positions leave 14% of the gallery width clear at either end for
the load-bearing columns. Wheel contacts, rooms and cannon anchors remain
coordinated with the assembly.
