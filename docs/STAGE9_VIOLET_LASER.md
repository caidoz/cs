# Stage nine violet discharge

The ninth castle retains its registered 1010 native pixel visible hull width, wider than stage eight's 1000. Its stone floor is 58 reference pixels deep; the right end is seated on the tower edge after horizontal registration rather than extending offscreen.

The violet laser has a 64 reference pixel body, a wide aura and a white core. Five bent electrical arcs gather into the muzzle during charging. During firing two jagged discharge paths wrap the beam, change every two ticks, and emit short side branches. The muzzle flare is larger. Attack timing and damage remain unchanged.

Rendering uses the existing flame beam texture with runtime colors and articulated segments; no additional bitmap asset is required.

Validation: turret presentation tests; normal Debug Win32 cs.exe build; live interior/width/platform review and turret charge/fire review at 640x850 and 640x1408.
