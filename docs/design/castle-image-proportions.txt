- Preserve source aspect ratio using one scale for both axes. Height may differ.

- Keep the deck's original aspect ratio and match its material to the castle.
- Independent X/Y scaling is permitted only for window/button frames and transient animation effects.
- Regression: tools/castles/verify_proportions.py captures all ten at 850 and 1408 heights.
