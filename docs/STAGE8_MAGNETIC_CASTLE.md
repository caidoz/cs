# Late castle width and stage eight magnetic pulse

Stages eight through ten normalize their visible hull width to 1000, 1010 and 1020 native pixels respectively. Previous alpha bounds were 934, 885 and 954. The horizontal transform centers the actual painted silhouette, and follows through rooms, hull, cover, floor, gun socket and wheel centers. Height and wheel radii remain unchanged.

Stages six onward have a 52 reference pixel deep floor around the characters' feet.

Stage eight uses a dedicated Magnetic attack: 26 tick muzzle charge, one large 72 pixel orbital plasma projectile travelling straight over 22 ticks, one damage application on arrival. Rotating electron rings, aura and fading trail distinguish it from the previous flame beam. At impact the orb expands and throws off sparks.

Asset: `Resources/res/turret_attack/magnetic-orb.png` (256x256 RGBA), built-in imagegen output resized for runtime. Registered as `TURRET_MAGNETIC_ORB_IMG`.
